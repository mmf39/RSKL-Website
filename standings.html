Team Stat Definitions

PAM (Points Above Median)

PAM represents the cumulative number of points a team has scored above the league median on its gamedays. 

PAM = (total points scored) - (total gameday medians)

apPAM (Average Percentage Points Above Median)

apPAM is the average percent by which a team exceeds the league median on each gameday. 

pPAM_i = percentage above median in game i
apPAM = (pPAM_1 + pPAM_2 + ... + pPAM_n) / n

tREL (Team REL)

tREL is the weighted average REL of players on a team. Players with more games played have more weight. 

tREL = Σ(REL_i × GP_i) / Σ(GP_i)
