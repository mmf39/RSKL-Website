C2S3 Draft Lottery

C2S3 Draft Rules

  • D1A) We will host a draft lottery to prevent tanking
  • D1B) If a team tanks, they will lose their 1st round pick
  • D1C) Tanking is described as, purposely sending in a lineup that will lose, not starting your best players, or captaining a player who is not competitive with the group.
  • D1D) C2S2 draft is an exception since ⅚ teams qualified for playoffs
  • D2) Each team will have 1 pick per round to start, and it can be traded to acquire either picks or player
  • D3A) In the first round each team will get 2 minutes to pick, before an auto
  • D3B) After the first round, each team will get 1 minute to pick, before an auto
  • D4) Expansion teams will participate in an expansion draft and regular draft
  • D5A) 4 teams will not make playoffs there odds at the number one overall pick will be as followed
  • 7th best: 10%
  • 8th best: 20%
  • 9th best: 30%
  • 10th best: 40%
